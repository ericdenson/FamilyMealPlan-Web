Our dynamic 7-day schedule keeps your family aligned. Swipe between past, present, and future weeks, tap into detailed recipe summaries, and organize your dinners without the stress.

18 Diet Profiles. Diet variety handled.

Choose from 18 built-in diet profiles—including Keto, Dairy-Free, Gluten-Free, Vegan, and even our ultra-budget-friendly "I'm Broke" profile. Our app automatically filters and highlights recipes that fit your family's preferences perfectly.

Just select your diet, exclude any specific ingredients your family dislikes or is allergic to, and let AI do the heavy lifting. Never accidentally plan a meal you can't eat again.

Smart consolidated shopping lists.

We automatically combine and aggregate all ingredients from every meal on your schedule into a single, clean checklist. The list dynamically groups items by grocery aisle, making your store runs incredibly fast and organized.

Ready to shop online? Copy your full list with a single tap, then open your favorite delivery app—like Walmart, Amazon Fresh, Instacart, Kroger, or Target—directly from the shopping screen. While it doesn't automatically load the items into your store's cart yet, it makes pasting and searching for your ingredients quick and painless.
