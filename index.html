MEET THE CREATORS

Why we built Family Meal Plan

One day, my wife Deanna was sharing how exhausted she was trying to find online recipes that fit her diet, schedule them for the week, and write out consolidated grocery lists by hand. Being a software engineer, I looked at her frustration and said, "I'll build an app for that." I built Family Meal Plan with everything she needed, added helpful features she didn't even ask for (like automatic meal suggestions), and completely removed the things she hated about other apps—like hidden cancel buttons and forced credit cards just to start a trial.

Eric & Deanna Denson Founders, Family Meal Plan
