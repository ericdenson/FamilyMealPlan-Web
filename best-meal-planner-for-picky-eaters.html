Why standard meal plans fail picky eaters

Made for "Imaginary Families"

Traditional planners push pre-made menus with complicated ingredients (like quinoa bowls or kale wraps). Picky kids refuse them, leaving you cooking two separate dinners anyway.

Ignoring Food Exclusions

If your child hates onions or is allergic to peanuts, most systems make you check every recipe line-by-line. One slip-up means a wasted dinner or a severe allergic reaction.

Intimidating Setup Hours

Getting a system set up to filter recipes to fit your family usually takes hours of typing, uploading, and tag configurations. Who has time for that?

Built for real kids & real kitchens

1. Smart Custom Exclusion Filters

Whether it's a severe peanut allergy or just a strong kid hatred for cilantro, Family Meal Plan has your back. Input specific ingredients on your profile, and they are automatically scrubbed from your suggestion engine and shopping lists.

You don't need to double-check recipe lists anymore—if you excluded it, it simply won't show up.

2. Seeds with Meals Your Family Already Loves

We don't force foreign menus on your table. You start by entering or scanning your family's favorite meals—like Grandma's Tuna Casserole, simple tacos, or standard chicken tenders. Our morning "Decide For Me" engine pulls suggestions directly from those rated favorites first, so the suggestion is a guaranteed hit.

3. 16 Built-in Diet Profiles

Need Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free, Low Carb, or Vegetarian options? Choose from 16 built-in dietary profiles to filter recommendations. Combined with your custom ingredient exclusions, the app tailors your plan to exactly what your picky eaters will eat—and ignores the rest.
