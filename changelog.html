August 11, 2026

Smart Grocery Aisle Overrides & Spice Extensions (v1.0.15)

We improved how the smart shopping list categorizes items to keep your store runs friction-free, and optimized how crawlers read the website:

  • Spices & Seasonings Fixed: Corrected an issue where ground spices (like ground cumin, paprika, turmeric, cloves, coriander, and allspice) fell through to the "Other" section. They are now correctly routed to the Pantry aisle.
  • Aisle Collisions Fixed: Added logic to keep baking mixes from landing in Dairy & Eggs, and corrected a word-match issue where rolled oats accidentally categorized into Bakery instead of Pantry.
  • Rich Schema Data: Added complete structured JSON-LD FAQ, Organization, and Product schemas so AI search agents correctly display our pricing and app specifications.
  • Descriptive Image Alt Text: Rewrote all mockup image descriptions to improve accessibility and image-search performance.
By Eric & Deanna Denson, Founders
July 10, 2026

Synchronized Pricing Tiers & Premium UI Features

To offer a lower entry cost barrier for families, we adjusted our subscription rates and refreshed the landing page experience:

  • New $64.99 Yearly Rate: Updated App Store Connect and Google Play Console base plans to synchronize the yearly "All In" subscription tier to its lowest rate ever.
  • Pricing Normalization: Reformatted the yearly plan card on the website to show its monthly equivalent ($5.41/mo billed yearly) with a "Save 40%" badge to match standard pricing periods.
  • Founder Block Section: Added our personal story section explaining exactly why we built Family Meal Plan to eliminate dinner-scramble friction.
By Eric Denson, Founder
June 20, 2026

Recipe Camera Scan & Diet Filters Launch

We launched the initial core experience of Family Meal Plan to eliminate kitchen planning fatigue:

  • Magic Photo Scanner: Point your camera at paper recipe pages to scan, digitalize, and format ingredients and steps automatically.
  • Built-in Diets & Allergens: Integrated 16 diet filters (Keto, Gluten-Free, dairy-free) and exclusions to remove unapproved ingredients from your dinner suggestions automatically.
  • Automatic Suggestion Scheduler: First-of-its-kind morning notification that decides dinner for you from recipes your family has rated and cooked.
By Eric Denson, Founder
