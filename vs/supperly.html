The Mental Load: Push vs. Pull

Supperly is a Pull system. It gives you a collaborative shared space to vote on recipes, input your pantry items, and manually build your calendar. But that requires your family to constantly think, coordinate, and choose. When it's 5:47 PM, the last thing you want is a group chat debate.

Family Meal Plan is a Push system. Every single morning, if you haven't scheduled a dinner, our app automatically decides one for you. It pulls first from your family's rated favorites and past meals, so it actually fits your table. If you don't like it, one tap shuffles it. You don't have to plan; dinner just decides itself.

Supperly's Way

Coordinate recipes, log every fridge ingredient, have the family vote, and manually plan calendars. Feels like extra work.

Our Way

Wake up. Your dinner suggestion is already decided, customized to your allergies and past likes. Tap once to swap if needed. Done.
