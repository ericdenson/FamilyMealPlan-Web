Frequently Asked Questions

Why is an automatic "decider" better for families?

Most planning systems fail because life gets busy, and you simply run out of time to make choices. Family Meal Plan removes this friction entirely by proactively choosing a meal your family loves every morning. You can always change it, but starting from a completed calendar is much easier than starting from a blank page.

Can I import my own recipes into Family Meal Plan?

Yes! This is one of our biggest advantages over NumYum. Our Magic AI Importer allows you to take a picture of any physical card or cookbook page, or paste a link from any website. Our software turns it into a clean, digital, searchable recipe instantly.

Is my recipe data private?

100% yes. We never sell your personal information or family recipes. Everything you upload and save is tied privately to your account so that it stays yours forever.
