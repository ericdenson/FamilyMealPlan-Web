Frequently Asked Questions

Why can't I upload my own recipes in Mealime?

Mealime is built around standardized recipes linked directly to their grocery list partnership systems. This makes custom inputs difficult. Family Meal Plan was built with flexibility in mind: we use advanced AI parser intelligence to read *your* personal recipes, extract ingredients, and map them to standard grocery sections automatically.

How does the morning decider engine work?

If your daily dinner block is empty, Family Meal Plan suggests a meal at 6:00 AM based on what you have historically liked, combined with your active diet exclusions. This ensures you wake up to a plan rather than a blank canvas, ending the daily dinner scramble.

What is "Pay What It's Worth" pricing?

We believe you know your family's budget best. After your 14-day free trial, you can choose the tier that matches your usage and support level—ranging from $5.99 to $12.99/mo, or choose our long-term annual option at $64.99/yr.
