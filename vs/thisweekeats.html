Dinner is about Connection, not Medical Data

ThisWeekEats treats dinner like a clinical regimen. It syncs with Continuous Glucose Monitors (CGMs), smart scales, and Apple HealthKit to track medical targets (often used by those managing medical or GLP-1 treatment diets). Because of this complex health integration, it has a high learning curve, requires constant syncs, and hides its pricing structure behind a registration paywall.

Family Meal Plan is built for real families. We don't want to scan your scale or link your medical trackers. We want to solve the simple, daily friction of feeding a family. Our app is warm, straightforward, and built by real parents for real kitchens. It helps you schedule dinners, digitalize handwritten family recipes, and automatically organize grocery store aisles.
