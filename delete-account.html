Account Deletion Request

If you no longer wish to use Family Meal Plan, you can request the permanent deletion of your account and all associated personal data.

Warning: Deleting your account is permanent. This action will erase your email address, all private recipes, custom meal plans, and grocery lists from our servers. This action cannot be undone.

Method 1: Delete via the App (Fastest)

You can instantly delete your account directly from within the Family Meal Plan mobile app:

  • Open the Family Meal Plan app on your device.
  • Tap on your Profile/Settings tab.
  • Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete Account.
  • Confirm your decision. Your data will be instantly erased.

Method 2: Request Deletion via Email

If you have already uninstalled the app and wish to have your account deleted, you can submit a manual deletion request by contacting our support team.

Please send an email to our support team from the email address associated with your Family Meal Plan account, requesting account deletion.

Email Support to Delete Account

Note: For manual requests, please allow up to 7 business days for our team to process the deletion. We may ask you to verify your identity before proceeding.
